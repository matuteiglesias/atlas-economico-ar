# Open decisions deliberately not hidden

1. **Policy-rate identity**: Argentina's operating framework has changed; the canonical `policy_rate` concept is retained while the exact technical series is deferred.
2. **FX market canon**: official, CCL and blue are represented; final Series registry must define precise source and timestamp conventions.
3. **REER methodology**: canonical concept is included; bilateral vs multilateral REER and price-index choices are deferred to measurement hardening.
4. **FX pass-through estimation**: the IMF provides a staff estimate and decomposition; a reproducible project-level rolling estimate requires a documented econometric methodology and should not be inferred from simple co-movement.
5. **Inflation-driver decomposition**: PlotIntent exists because the IMF demonstrates explanatory value, but reproducing its model decomposition requires either IMF methodology/data or our own versioned model.
6. **Money-demand projection gap**: the indicator is valid semantically, but the BCRA projection path must be located and versioned later.
7. **BCRA FX operations**: included as a boundary-port concept because it is necessary for nominal policy; reserve-stock adequacy belongs to the External & Financial Constraint slice.
8. **Credibility**: treated as a latent semantic concept measured through multiple proxies, not as a directly observed scalar.
