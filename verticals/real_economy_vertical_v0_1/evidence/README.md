## IMF findings used

- Recent Developments para. 5: real GDP growth was supported by stronger private demand, recovering investment and robust exports, especially energy, mining and agriculture, while the recovery remained uneven and construction/manufacturing lagged.
- Figure 3, *Recent Economic and Social Developments*: explicitly juxtaposes aggregate activity with construction/retail/manufacturing versus agriculture/fishing/mining/energy, and presents private/public consumption, GFCF, exports and imports as the demand-side growth decomposition.
- Structural-policy discussion: investment, productivity, resource reallocation and tradable-sector capacity are relevant medium-run mechanisms, but not automatically observed causal effects.
